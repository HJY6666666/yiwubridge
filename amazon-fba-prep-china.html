Amazon FBA prep in Yiwu, China

YiwuBridge helps Amazon sellers turn supplier cartons into a shipment that follows the buyer’s FBA plan: receiving, label coordination, carton checks, consolidation, packing evidence and freight handoff.

Direct answer: Amazon FBA prep works best when product, SKU, label and carton requirements are confirmed before goods leave each supplier. YiwuBridge can coordinate that work in Yiwu, then consolidate and hand over cargo to the buyer’s selected shipping route.

What an FBA-ready shipment needs

Amazon receiving requirements are set in the seller’s shipment plan, while the practical work happens across suppliers, packaging teams and freight. Before dispatch, buyers need a single checklist for SKU separation, barcode files, carton labels, carton dimensions, weight limits, packaging instructions and destination details.

A local coordination point is useful when orders come from several Yiwu, 1688 or factory suppliers. Rather than allowing every supplier to prepare cartons differently, the buyer can set agreed checkpoints before the shipment moves.

Controls for mixed-supplier FBA orders

Mixed-SKU orders often lose margin through duplicated local deliveries, inconsistent carton marks and unnecessary air volume. YiwuBridge can receive goods from more than one supplier, match work to the approved packing brief and arrange buyer-approved repacking where appropriate. For quality checkpoints before final payment, see our Yiwu quality control service.

Product condition and FBA compliance are different checks. An inspection can document visible product, quantity and packaging issues, while the shipment plan governs labels and Amazon receiving instructions. Keeping both records together makes approval clearer.

What to send with an Amazon FBA prep request

  • Product links, supplier names or purchase-order references
  • Marketplace and destination fulfillment center details
  • SKU list, product quantities and label files
  • Carton dimensions or packing limits, if already known
  • Packaging, bundle, insert or warning-label requirements
  • Target collection date and freight-forwarder contact

Amazon FBA prep FAQ

Can YiwuBridge consolidate several China suppliers for Amazon FBA?

Yes. We can receive mixed supplier orders in Yiwu, verify agreed quantities and carton details, consolidate cargo and coordinate the buyer’s FBA preparation requirements before freight handoff.

Can you apply FNSKU and carton labels?

YiwuBridge can coordinate buyer-provided label files and approved placement instructions. Send the current shipment plan and label requirements before the goods arrive so the work can be checked against the right version.

Do you arrange the international freight?

We coordinate the export handoff and can work with your chosen forwarder. Freight route, customs responsibility and delivery terms should be confirmed directly with the freight provider before dispatch.
