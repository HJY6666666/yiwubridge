MOQ characteristics

Ask for the quote basis first

MOQ is often controlled by color, size run, yarn or fabric availability, and packaging. Ask for a size-color matrix before confirming a quote.

RFQ details that speed up comparison

  • Product links, photos, specification or sample reference.
  • Quantity by style, color, size or model.
  • Target price, destination country, packaging and labeling needs.
  • Required testing, inspection, FBA preparation or delivery deadline.

Buyer pitfalls to avoid

Before deposit or production

  • Comparing apparel accessories without a material composition or size specification.
  • Approving a color without a physical swatch or production-lot reference.
  • Ignoring size ratio, inner-pack ratio and carton labeling until after production.
