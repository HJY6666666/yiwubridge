MOQ characteristics

Ask for the quote basis first

MOQ can be set by fabric roll, color lot, design, vehicle model or retail pack. Obtain a written specification and packing ratio before approving samples.

RFQ details that speed up comparison

  • Product links, photos, specification or sample reference.
  • Quantity by style, color, size or model.
  • Target price, destination country, packaging and labeling needs.
  • Required testing, inspection, FBA preparation or delivery deadline.

Buyer pitfalls to avoid

Before deposit or production

  • Treating imported-goods availability as proof of brand authorization or export rights.
  • Ordering textiles without confirming composition, dimensions and dye lot.
  • Ordering automotive accessories without fitment, compatibility and packaging checks.
