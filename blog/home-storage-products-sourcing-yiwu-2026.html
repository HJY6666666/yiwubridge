1. Why Yiwu works for home storage product sourcing

Storage products sell across Amazon, Shopify stores, discount shops, supermarkets, home improvement stores and local retailers. The category is broad: plastic storage bins, drawer organizers, closet racks, foldable fabric boxes, hooks, hangers, laundry baskets, under-bed storage, bathroom shelves and kitchen organizers. 3. QC checklist before balance payment

Storage products look simple, but small defects create many after-sales problems. For example, a plastic bin may crack during shipping, a fabric box may smell, a shelf may miss screws, or a hanger hook may bend under weight. Buyers should not approve balance payment only from supplier catalog photos.

  • Confirm exact dimensions, color, material, thickness and set quantity against the approved sample.
  • Check surface defects: scratches, stains, uneven color, sharp edges, burrs, rust, odor or deformation.
  • Test functional points: lid fit, folding stability, handle strength, wheels, clips, hooks and assembly parts.
  • Check packaging: inner bag, barcode position, instruction sheet, carton mark, carton quantity and drop risk.
  • Take warehouse photos and short videos before goods leave China.

For higher-value orders, define an AQL inspection plan or at least a practical sample check ratio before production finishes. YiwuBridge can help prepare photo evidence and defect notes before shipment.

4. Packaging and consolidation matter more than buyers expect

Home storage products can be bulky. Poor carton planning increases CBM and makes shipping cost look worse than the product price. Before confirming an order, check whether products can be nested, folded or packed in sets. For e-commerce sellers, confirm barcode labels, SKU stickers, warning labels and outer carton marks.

If you are buying from three or more suppliers, warehouse consolidation is usually more efficient than shipping each supplier separately. A Yiwu warehouse can receive cartons, count quantities, combine SKUs, remove unnecessary packing where safe, and prepare cleaner export documents.

5. RFQ template for home storage products

Send this information when requesting a quote:

  • Product photo or reference link.
  • Material, size, color, thickness, load capacity and set quantity.
  • Target order quantity and whether mixed SKUs are acceptable.
  • Packaging style: bulk pack, retail box, polybag, label, barcode or private label.
  • Destination country, delivery address type, deadline and preferred shipping method.

The clearer your RFQ, the easier it is to compare real suppliers instead of comparing incomplete prices. For mixed home storage orders, ask for product cost, local handling, service fee, inspection scope and shipping estimate as separate lines.
