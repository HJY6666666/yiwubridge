General merchandise procurement

General merchandise procurement in China: build a mixed household assortment

General merchandise buyers usually win through a useful assortment, dependable retail packing and sensible shipment planning. The challenge is not finding one item; it is turning many small household SKUs into a controlled, profitable order.

Direct answer: A mixed household assortment is easier to procure when every supplier works from one buying brief that sets the SKU list, color mix, MOQ, packaging, carton marks and destination requirements before production begins.

Plan the assortment before contacting suppliers

Start with a narrow assortment that serves one retail use case, such as cleaning, kitchen basics, laundry or bathroom organization. Give every item a SKU, target quantity, color options and retail-pack requirement. This prevents a supplier from quoting a visually similar item with a different size or material.

Compare suppliers on landed-order details

Ask for product price, MOQ, inner pack, master-carton quantity, outer-carton dimensions, gross weight and lead time in one comparison sheet. General merchandise is often low-value and volume-sensitive, so carton data should be treated as part of the quotation rather than a freight-stage detail.

Use practical QC points

Most household products can be checked with clear visible criteria: color mix, count, material consistency, odor, sharp edges, moving parts, accessory completeness, retail labels and carton marks. Product claims that depend on performance should have an agreed test method before production. Use the quality control service page to define the local review process.

Consolidate with a receiving plan

Mixed orders need a receiving list that records supplier, SKU, expected carton count, carton mark, quantity and packaging note. This creates a cleaner handoff from supplier delivery to warehouse consolidation and export shipping. Use our procurement brief template before sending a large mixed-SKU inquiry.

Frequently asked questions

What is general merchandise sourcing from China?

It is the procurement of a mixed range of everyday retail goods, often across cleaning, kitchen, laundry, bathroom, storage and seasonal categories, from more than one supplier.

How can buyers control MOQ across many household SKUs?

Confirm whether the MOQ applies by color, design, inner box, master carton or total order. Build a list that shows the exact assortment and carton split before placing a deposit.
