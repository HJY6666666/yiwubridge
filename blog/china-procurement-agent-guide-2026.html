China Procurement Agent Guide 2026: Services, Fees, QC and Shipping

A good procurement agent should not be only a messenger between buyer and supplier. For importers, Amazon sellers, wholesalers and small brands, the real value is control: clearer quotations, safer supplier checks, visible quality evidence, mixed-order consolidation and predictable shipping decisions.

1. What a China procurement agent should actually do

A procurement agent in China helps turn a buyer's product idea, link list or RFQ into a controlled buying process. The work starts before any deposit is paid: clarify product requirements, compare supplier options, confirm MOQ and lead time, check whether the quote is complete, and identify where quality or shipping risk may appear.

For Yiwu and nearby supply hubs, this is especially useful when a buyer wants many small SKUs from different suppliers. A local team can check the offline market, compare 1688 or Yiwugo listings with real stock, receive cartons in one warehouse, and prepare one shipment instead of many fragmented parcels.

2. Procurement agent services: what should be included?

Stage What the agent should do Buyer evidence to request
RFQ review Clarify product name, size, material, target price, quantity, packaging and destination country. Confirmed RFQ sheet, missing-detail notes, product-link comparison.
Supplier search Compare Yiwu Market, Yiwugo, 1688, Alibaba or nearby factory options. Supplier shortlist, MOQ, lead time, payment terms, quote scope.

Export shipping Coordinate express, air, sea, rail or FBA delivery based on timeline and budget. Shipping plan, estimated CBM/weight, invoice/packing list, tracking handoff.

3. How China procurement agent fees should be quoted

The safest pricing model is transparent. A buyer should see product cost, domestic transport, inspection, warehouse handling, packaging work, service fee and international freight as separate lines where possible. If an agent promises "free sourcing" but refuses to show quote details, the hidden cost may be inside supplier markups, inflated freight or poor quality control.

For standard mixed-category sourcing, a clear 3% to 5% service fee on confirmed shipments is easier to audit than unclear rebates. The buyer should also ask whether sample handling, product photos, FBA labels, carton relabeling and storage days are included or quoted separately.

4. Risk controls before you pay a supplier

Before deposit, ask the procurement agent to confirm supplier identity, beneficiary details, quote scope, MOQ, production time, sample standard, packaging method and what happens if inspection fails. For high-risk categories, the buyer should also review destination-market compliance with a qualified compliance party.

For Yiwu-style mixed orders, the most common problems are wrong colors, missing SKUs, thin cartons, changed packaging, unclear barcode requirements, supplier delays and split shipments. These can be reduced when the agent provides warehouse photos, packing lists, defect evidence and one final shipment plan before balance payment.

5. RFQ template for a China procurement agent

Send a short but complete brief. A useful RFQ should include:

  • Product name, photos, links or drawings.
  • Target quantity, target price and acceptable MOQ.
  • Material, size, color, logo, packaging and barcode requirements.
  • Destination country, sales channel, deadline and preferred shipping method.
  • Whether you need samples, QC photos, FBA prep, private-label packaging or consolidation.
