MOQ characteristics

Ask for the quote basis first

MOQ is commonly set by color, mold, printed artwork or pack configuration. Mixed designs may be possible only when the buyer accepts supplier-standard packaging.

RFQ details that speed up comparison

  • Product links, photos, specification or sample reference.
  • Quantity by style, color, size or model.
  • Target price, destination country, packaging and labeling needs.
  • Required testing, inspection, FBA preparation or delivery deadline.

Buyer pitfalls to avoid

Before deposit or production

  • Using supplier marketing claims without checking product category and destination requirements.
  • Approving print colors from a screen instead of a physical proof.
  • Ignoring small-parts, ink, cosmetic-contact or eyewear labeling questions.
