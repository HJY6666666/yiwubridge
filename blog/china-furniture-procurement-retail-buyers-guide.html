Furniture procurement

China furniture procurement: a buyer guide for retail and e-commerce orders

Furniture procurement requires a tighter brief than most small consumer goods. A photo can hide the material grade, hardware quality, assembly tolerance and carton volume that decide whether the order is profitable after delivery.

Direct answer: China furniture procurement works best when the buyer confirms the product specification, material, hardware, assembly method, protective packing and actual carton dimensions before approving production or freight.

Choose the right furniture product for the order

Retailers should start with products that have a clear assembly method and repeatable carton plan: shelving, side tables, compact cabinets, foldable pieces, storage furniture and selected office accessories. Decide early whether the item will ship assembled, partially assembled or flat packed. The decision affects damage risk, warehouse labor and the buyer’s final delivery cost.

Build a procurement brief before comparing prices

Give every supplier the same material, dimensions, finish, color, hardware and packaging request. Ask for the quote, MOQ, lead time, carton size, gross weight and assembly information together. A lower factory price is not comparable if one quote omits a stronger carton, the right hardware or required protective inserts.

Approve samples and inspect production evidence

Keep an approved sample or detailed photo record for the finish, edges, hardware, fabric and packaging. Before final payment, agree which points need evidence: product dimensions, function, assembly fit, color, accessory count, corner protection and carton marks. See our pre-shipment inspection checklist for a reusable inspection brief.

Control carton volume before booking freight

Furniture margins are sensitive to carton dimensions. Obtain actual outer-carton measurements rather than relying on estimated product size. For mixed orders, a local warehouse can combine cartons, record receiving details and coordinate buyer-approved protection or labeling before the freight handoff. Frequently asked questions

What should a furniture procurement RFQ include?

Include product photos or drawings, dimensions, material and finish, color, hardware, assembly method, quantity, packaging requirement, destination country and target delivery date.

Can China furniture suppliers handle small test orders?

Some suppliers can support a smaller trial order, but the MOQ can apply by model, color, fabric, carton or hardware set. Confirm the exact MOQ rule before comparing suppliers.
