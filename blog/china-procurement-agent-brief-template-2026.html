How to Brief a China Procurement Agent: A 10-Detail Template for Better Supplier Quotes

A vague message such as “please find this product” usually creates vague quotes. Use this practical brief to help a China procurement agent compare suppliers on the product, quantity, packaging, quality and delivery scope that actually matters to your order.

Quick answer: send your agent a product reference, quantity, acceptable specification, target price range, packaging and label needs, destination, timing, quality checks, sample plan and decision constraints. The agent can then return comparable supplier options instead of several prices for different products.

Why a clear brief produces more useful quotes

Suppliers can only quote what they understand. If the product photo, material, size, packaging and quantity are unclear, each supplier may quote a different grade or configuration. A low price then tells you very little because it is not comparable.

A procurement agent should use your brief to clarify the buying route and normalize supplier replies. You do not need every technical detail before contacting an agent. You do need to separate what is fixed from what can be suggested, so the agent knows where alternatives are acceptable.

The 10 details to include in a procurement-agent brief

DetailWhat to sendWhy the agent needs it
1. Product referenceLinks, photos, drawings or a sample videoConfirms which product version you mean
2. Fixed specificationMaterial, size, color, function and acceptable alternativesStops suppliers from quoting different grades
3. QuantityTrial quantity, order quantity per SKU and expected reorder volumeSets a realistic MOQ and price comparison
4. Target priceA workable price range and whether packaging or freight is includedHelps the agent rule out unsuitable options early
5. Branding and packagingLogo, barcode, insert, retail box, carton mark or FBA-label needsShows whether a supplier can support the full scope
6. Destination and deadlineCountry, delivery target and preferred shipping route if knownLinks product readiness with export planning
7. Supplier scopeWhether market, 1688, factory or mixed options are acceptableGuides the supplier search instead of limiting it to one channel
8. Sample planWhether you need physical samples, photos, video or a small pilot orderCreates an approval point before bulk production
9. Quality and complianceCritical defects, test requirements, labels and buyer-platform rulesTurns “good quality” into checkable evidence
10. Decision constraintsBudget, must-have features, prohibited materials and your preferred contact methodLets the agent prioritize the right questions and next action

Copy-and-send RFQ template

Paste the following outline into your inquiry. Replace the brackets with what you know, and leave a note where you want supplier suggestions.

Product: [name, link/photo, key use case]
Specification: [material, size, color, function; acceptable alternatives]
Quantity: [trial quantity / bulk quantity per SKU]
Target price: [currency and range; say what is included]
Branding and packaging: [logo, label, barcode, insert, retail box, carton mark]
Destination and timing: [country, required arrival date, preferred shipping if known]
Supplier scope: [Yiwu Market / 1688 / factory / open to suggestions]
Sample and quality: [sample plan, critical checks, platform or compliance needs]
Decision point: [what you need back: shortlist, sample, quote comparison or shipping plan]
Contact: [name, company, email and WhatsApp]

Ask the agent to return a comparable supplier shortlist

A helpful reply is more than a list of prices. Ask for the supplier type, product differences, MOQ, unit price, sample cost, packaging scope, lead time and any open questions. If the order involves several suppliers, ask how samples, quality control, warehouse receiving and consolidation would be handled.

Compare the same scope before choosing the lowest price

Compare price only after you confirm the same material, dimensions, packaging, MOQ, quality expectation and trade terms. A lower unit price can become more expensive if it excludes the packaging, sample, domestic delivery, inspection or correction work your order needs. Brief template FAQ

Can I start with only a photo?

Yes. Add the intended use, approximate size, quantity and your target market. Tell the agent which details are flexible so they can find practical alternatives.

Should I give a target price?

Yes, when you have one. A range is more useful than an unexplained single number. State whether it includes packaging, domestic delivery or freight so quotes can be compared fairly.

Is an agent quote a final order confirmation?

No. Treat the first quote as a comparison point. Confirm sample, specification, packaging, payment terms, quality requirements and shipping plan before approving a bulk order.
