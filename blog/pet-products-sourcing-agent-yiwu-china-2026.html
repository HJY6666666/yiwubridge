1. Why pet products are a good fit for a Yiwu sourcing agent

Pet accessories are often small, visual, SKU-heavy and packaging-sensitive. A seller may want five collar colors, three grooming tools, two pet bowls, a travel bag and a seasonal toy in one trial order. That is exactly where a local sourcing agent can add value: compare suppliers, confirm stock, collect cartons, check basic quality, consolidate mixed items and prepare export documents.

The opportunity is also supported by market demand. APPA reported that the U.S. pet industry reached $158 billion in 2025 and expected continued growth in 2026. Grand View Research also projected U.S. pet hard goods growth through 2030, including categories such as bedding, toys, collars, leashes, grooming tools, feeding supplies, cleaning and travel items. These are the product types overseas buyers often ask a China sourcing agent to compare.

The risk is that pet products look simple until a buyer checks real use. A weak buckle, chemical smell, sharp plastic edge, wrong barcode, thin carton or missing warning label can turn a profitable SKU into a refund problem. Treat pet supplies as a category that needs specification control, not only cheap prices.

3. MOQ and private label planning

Do not ask for the lowest MOQ before the specification is clear. Many pet accessories can be tested with mixed colors or ready-stock items, but private-label packaging, custom colors and material changes will raise MOQ. A practical buying plan separates what can be tested now from what should wait until sales are proven.

Buying stage Common setup What to control
Trial order Ready-stock SKUs, mixed colors, simple label or sticker Real product quality, package fit, shipping cost and customer feedback
Small private label Logo sticker, hang tag, insert card, branded carton or poly bag Artwork version, barcode, warning copy, carton mark and photo proof
Repeat order Confirmed supplier, stable material, fixed color mix, improved packaging Defect rate, lead time, carton CBM, landed cost and reorder timing
Custom development New mold, custom fabric, special function or exclusive design Tooling ownership, sample rounds, test requirements and IP documentation

A sourcing agent should quote product cost and packaging cost separately. This prevents the buyer from approving a low unit price and later discovering that hang tags, inserts, cartons or Amazon labels were not included.

4. Pet product QC checklist before shipment

  • Material and smell: check obvious odor, surface finish, fabric feel and material consistency.
  • Size and fit: compare product measurements with the approved size chart or sample.
  • Strength points: review stitching, buckles, zippers, clips, seams and load-bearing parts.
  • Small parts: identify detachable parts, sharp edges, loose bells, squeakers or decorations.
  • Packaging: confirm SKU label, barcode, warning text, instruction card, color label and carton marks.
  • Quantity and mix: count color and size ratios, not only total cartons.
  • Photo evidence: require carton photos, product detail photos and defect photos before balance payment.

If a product is designed for chewing, feeding, electrical use, medication, supplements or children-like play, ask a compliance specialist about the destination market before production. A sourcing agent can coordinate samples, packaging and supplier documents, but legal compliance decisions should be reviewed by qualified professionals for the buyer's market.

5. FBA, warehouse and shipping prep

Pet products often include many small SKUs, which makes warehouse discipline important. Before goods move from Yiwu or nearby suppliers to international shipping, the buyer should confirm SKU separation, carton marks, barcode labels, packing list, actual weight, CBM and destination delivery requirement.

For Amazon FBA, confirm whether each unit needs an FNSKU label, suffocation warning, poly bag thickness, bundle label, expiration date or country-specific compliance mark. For independent-store warehouses, confirm carton label format, pallet requirements and appointment rules. The cheapest freight quote is not useful if the shipment is rejected at the destination warehouse.

6. Pet products RFQ template

To get a faster quote, send your sourcing agent the following information in one message:

  • Product name, photos or links
  • Target size, material, color mix and quantity
  • Target price or current supplier price
  • Packaging requirement: neutral, sticker, hang tag, insert card or retail box
  • Destination country and shipping method preference
  • Amazon FBA, Shopify warehouse or wholesale delivery requirement
  • Any required test report, warning label or compliance document
