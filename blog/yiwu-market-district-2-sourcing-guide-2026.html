MOQ characteristics

Ask for the quote basis first

MOQ often follows the model, color, material or logo process. A low display-order MOQ may not apply to custom packaging, electrical configuration or private-label production.

RFQ details that speed up comparison

  • Product links, photos, specification or sample reference.
  • Quantity by style, color, size or model.
  • Target price, destination country, packaging and labeling needs.
  • Required testing, inspection, FBA preparation or delivery deadline.

Buyer pitfalls to avoid

Before deposit or production

  • Comparing hardware without matching material grade, dimensions and finish.
  • Assuming a plug or voltage configuration is suitable for the destination market.
  • Skipping function checks, drop checks or carton-strength checks for heavier products.
