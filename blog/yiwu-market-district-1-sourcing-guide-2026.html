MOQ characteristics

Ask for the quote basis first

Many styles are quoted by design, color or display pack. Ask whether the MOQ is per SKU, per color, per carton, or across a mixed order before comparing prices.

RFQ details that speed up comparison

  • Product links, photos, specification or sample reference.
  • Quantity by style, color, size or model.
  • Target price, destination country, packaging and labeling needs.
  • Required testing, inspection, FBA preparation or delivery deadline.

Buyer pitfalls to avoid

Before deposit or production

  • Treating a booth sample as proof of repeat production capacity.
  • Comparing jewelry or toys by unit price without confirming material, finish, packaging and test needs.
  • Mixing fragile craft items with heavy cartons without a separate packing plan.
