FAQ

Yiwu sourcing agent questions

Learn how YiwuBridge supports global buyers who need a local China buying agent for supplier search, product sourcing, inspection, warehouse consolidation, and international shipping.

What does a Yiwu sourcing agent do?

A Yiwu sourcing agent helps you find suppliers, compare quotations, negotiate details, check samples, inspect goods, consolidate orders, and arrange export shipping from China.

How does YiwuBridge's sourcing process work?

Send product links, photos or specifications with quantity, target price and destination. We compare suitable suppliers, confirm MOQ and packaging, coordinate samples and quality checks, then consolidate goods and support export shipping after you approve the order.

Can YiwuBridge consolidate products from different suppliers?

Yes. We can receive goods from multiple suppliers, check quantities, relabel or repack products, and consolidate them into one export shipment.

Which product categories can YiwuBridge source?

We source kitchenware, household goods, fashion accessories, toys, stationery, tools, lighting, packaging, home decor, baby products, pet supplies, seasonal gifts, and custom products.

What is your service fee?

We offer free sourcing and quotation. We only charge a 3% - 5% service fee based on your total order value when the shipment is confirmed. No hidden costs.

What payment methods do you accept?

To ensure transactional security and minimize costs for our clients, we support Standard International Wire Transfers (T/T), Local Bank Transfers (USD/EUR/GBP via XTransfer/WorldFirst), and Alibaba Trade Assurance. For small sample payments under $500, PayPal is also accepted.

Can you ship to Amazon FBA or independent store warehouses?

Yes! We are highly experienced with Amazon FBA standards. We provide free carton labeling, SKU barcoding, and direct pallet or parcel shipping to global fulfillment centers.

Can I start with only product photos or Yiwugo and 1688 links?

Yes. Send product photos, Yiwugo links, 1688 links, Alibaba links, specs, target price, quantity, destination country, and deadline. We will check supplier options and prepare a practical quotation.

Can YiwuBridge source from 1688, Taobao, Yiwugo and Weidian?

Yes. Send us 1688, Taobao, Yiwugo, Weidian, Alibaba, or competitor product links. We compare online listings with offline Yiwu market suppliers and local factories, then report MOQ, lead time, packaging notes, and shipping options.

How do you verify a China supplier before payment?

We check supplier identity, product category fit, quotation consistency, sample quality, payment beneficiary details, packaging requirements, and inspection evidence before recommending deposit or balance payment.

Can I start with a small pilot order before bulk purchasing?

Yes. Many Yiwu categories support mixed trial orders. We help you test samples, packaging, QC standards, and real shipping cost before committing to a larger bulk order.

How do you keep sourcing fees transparent?

We separate product cost, service fee, inspection, local handling, and freight. Our usual service fee is 3% - 5% on confirmed shipments, with no hidden supplier kickbacks or inflated shipping markups.

Can I send a formal RFQ with drawings or a PDF sourcing list?

Yes. Submit the RFQ form first, then email images, drawings, PDF lists, or specification files to hujiyuan0721@gmail.com. Include your name or WhatsApp number so we can match the files with your inquiry.

Is YiwuBridge a legally registered company?
