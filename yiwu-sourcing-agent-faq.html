Frequently asked questions

What does YiwuBridge do as a Yiwu sourcing agent?

YiwuBridge compares suppliers, clarifies MOQ and terms, coordinates samples and QC, receives goods in a Yiwu warehouse, consolidates cartons and supports export shipping or Amazon FBA preparation.

Does YiwuBridge source from Yiwu Market and 1688?

Yes. We can research suitable options from Yiwu International Trade City, 1688, Taobao and factory-direct networks, then compare quotations against the same product, quantity, packaging and destination requirements.

How much does YiwuBridge charge?

Initial sourcing and quotation are free. YiwuBridge states a transparent 3%–5% service fee on confirmed order value, with the scope agreed before the order proceeds.

Can YiwuBridge consolidate mixed orders and support Amazon FBA?

Yes. We can receive mixed SKUs, verify agreed carton details, consolidate cargo, arrange buyer-approved repacking, and coordinate barcode, FNSKU, carton-mark and pallet preparation.

What should I send for a sourcing quote?

Send a product link, photo or drawing, target quantity, destination country, target price, packaging or labeling needs, timing and required quality or compliance checks.
